Nicety Auto Services is a mobile detailing and paint correction company serving Wichita, Kansas and the surrounding area. Owner Kyle Rothrock and the team do full interior and exterior detailing, paint correction, ceramic coating, starlight headliner installation, window tint, vehicle wraps, and audio system installation — all performed at the customer's home or office, with no shop drop-off required. Nicety Auto Services can be reached by phone or text at (316) 208-3234, by email at nicetymobile@gmail.com, or online at nicetyautowichita.com. Appointments can be booked directly through the site.
