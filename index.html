What is a starlight headliner?

Fiber optic points in the roof lining that make the inside of your car look like a night sky. Nicety designs and installs them in Wichita.

What’s the difference between paint correction and ceramic coating?

Paint correction polishes out swirls and light scratches to restore gloss. Ceramic coating is a protective layer applied afterward so that finish stays glossy and is easier to clean.
